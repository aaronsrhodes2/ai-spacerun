Version: 0.21.0
Date: 2026-11-21
  Features:
    - The Skippy panel: the conversation, always up on the right of the screen where the remote view leaves room, with
      the player's lines, Skippy's and purple's replies and voice lines as they happen, the working light, and a text box.
      Keyboard only: the Talk to Skippy key (Enter by default) puts the cursor in it, Enter sends and keeps it there.
      panel_type and panel_read let the bridge and tests type and read exactly as the player does.
    - Spider commander (map setting, off by default, can be switched on in a running game): the player lives in remote
      view and places ghosts; the character rides a spidertron that the assistant drives. A force with no spidertron gets
      one once, with roboports, power, shields, construction robots and a building kit (allowance 'commander-kit').
      Tools: spiders, spider_go (to a spot or place, or follow), spider_tend (a timed job that walks to ghosts the
      roboport network cannot reach); ghost_requests with spider writes the ghosts' needs into the spider's own requests.
    - Quality with skippy-quality 0.2.0: one quality module gives a machine's output the best quality researched, slowly
      (tier 1 3x the time, tier 2 2x, tier 3 1.5x); extra quality modules are taken out and spilled for the robots; the
      assistant's ghosts are placed at the best quality. Not yet: a machine holding its module out while nothing asks.
    - The map shows spidertrons and cars as '&'.

Version: 0.20.1
Date: 2026-11-21
  Bugfixes:
    - The avatar mines again on a fresh map: Factorio 2.1 turns hand mining off unless the target is selected, so the
      mining job selects it, and it walks half a tile inside its reach (at 2.66 of 2.7 tiles nothing was mined).
    - lookup on an item says which resources it is mined from, and plan_production takes mined ore as raw: on Nauvis it

    - lookup on a machine says how to get the time of one craft (the recipe's seconds divided by the crafting speed).
    - design_deploy with build_now says why pieces stay ghosts: ground to lay first (landfill over water), or marked
      trees and rocks; it had always blamed trees.

Version: 0.20.0
Date: 2026-11-20
  Features:
    - Designs: a blueprint library the model owns, as the source of what stands on the map. design_save writes a design
      from pieces, captures an area or imports a string, each save a new version with a note; design_edit swaps
      buildings or recipes; designs lists them with cost, deployments and history; design_export gives the string.
    - Deployments: design_deploy places a version as tagged ghosts for the robots, design_tile repeats one over a grid
      or line fixed to the map, design_redeploy moves a deployment to another version (upgrade orders when only names or
      recipes changed; otherwise everything of ours in its footprint is marked for deconstruction and rebuilt, so no old
      piece stays in a gap), design_undeploy takes one away, deployments lists them, what_is reads a tile's design.
    - Places: places, mark_place and forget_place read and write the force's map tags as names; find_placement and
      design_deploy take 'at' a place. Only the mod's own tags are moved or removed.
    - Cron: cron_list, cron_add, cron_change and cron_report keep every timed job in one list with its purpose, what good
      and bad runs look like and when it can retire; a job that starts failing or recovers is news for the model.
